IN CASE OF SICKNESS / INFECTION
• Measure glucose frequently, check for urine 
ketones if glucose >250 mg%
• Drink plenty of ﬂuids, monitor urine output
 
• Eat small light meals 4-5 times/day
• In addition to usual insulin doses, take extra 
regular insulin s.c. every 6 hourly (10-15% of total 
daily insulin dose)
• If glucose not falling, excess vomiting, low urine 
output, high or rising ketone, admit the patient
DKA MANAGEMENT
• As per STW on Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)
• Symptoms and signs: Sweating, hunger, tremors, 
irritability, weakness, drowsiness / seizures / 
unconsciousness (late stage)
• Diagnosis: Mild / moderate: glucose <70 mg% with 
or without symptoms
• Severe hypoglycemia: coma / seizures / inability to 
treat oneself
• Treatment: If glucose <70 mg% take 3 tsf glucose 
powder or sugar; if severe: caregiver should give inj. 
glucagon 1 mg s.c./ i.m. OTHERWISE IMMEDIATELY 
take to hospital for intravenous glucose injection (1-2 
ml/kg of 25% dextrose)
• Prevention: Identify mismatch of food, exercise, 
insulin
