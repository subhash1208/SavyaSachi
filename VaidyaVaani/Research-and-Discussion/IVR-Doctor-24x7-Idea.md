IDEA: IVR Doctor 24/7 (VaidyaVaani)
===============================================

THE "ONE-LINER":
Name: VaidyaVaani (वैद्यवाणी - "Doctor's Voice")
Pitch: An AI-powered IVR system that any Indian can call from ANY phone 
       (feature phone, smartphone, landline) to get instant health triage, 
       symptom assessment, and care navigation — in their native language, 
       24/7, without internet or literacy.

===============================================
THE "BHARAT" PAIN POINT
===============================================

"Let me tell you what happens at 2 AM in a village in Bihar. A mother's 
child has high fever and is vomiting. She has no smartphone. No internet. 
The nearest PHC is 15 km away and closed at night. She doesn't know if 
this is serious or can wait till morning. She can't read. She can't type. 
She can't use an app. All she has is a ₹1,500 feature phone with a SIM card.

Right now, her only options are:
1. Wake up the village quack (jhola-chaap doctor)
2. Wait till morning and hope the child survives
3. Spend ₹500 on an auto to the district hospital (2 hours away)

What if she could just DIAL A NUMBER and speak to an AI doctor in Bhojpuri 
who tells her: 'Didi, yeh dehydration lag raha hai. ORS banao abhi — ek 
chammach namak, 6 chammach cheeni, ek litre paani. Agar 2 ghante mein 
theek na ho, toh 108 call karo. Main yaad dilaungi.'

That's VaidyaVaani. Healthcare through a phone call. No app. No internet. 
No literacy. Just your voice."

AGENTIC CAPABILITIES (WHAT MAKES IT "AGENTIC")
===============================================

The AI doesn't just TALK — it ACTS:

1. TRIAGE AGENT
   - Listens to symptoms
   - Asks clarifying questions
   - Assesses severity (Green/Yellow/Red)
   - Decides next action autonomously

2. TREATMENT ADVISOR AGENT
   - Provides home remedies for minor issues
   - Gives first-aid instructions
   - Explains medication dosages (if prescribed)
   - Warns about danger signs to watch

3. EMERGENCY DISPATCH AGENT
   - Detects critical symptoms (chest pain, stroke signs, severe bleeding)
   - Autonomously triggers 108/112 ambulance
   - Shares patient location (if available) or guides to nearest landmark
   - Pre-notifies hospital about incoming case

4. FOLLOW-UP AGENT
   - Schedules callback after X hours
   - Checks if patient improved
   - Escalates if condition worsened
   - Maintains continuity of care

5. FAMILY ALERT AGENT
   - Sends SMS to registered family members in emergencies
   - Provides location and condition summary
   - Enables remote family to coordinate care

6. REFERRAL AGENT
   - Identifies nearest appropriate facility (PHC, CHC, District Hospital)
   - Checks bed availability (if integrated)
   - Provides directions and contact numbers
   - Can book appointment at eSanjeevani for video follow-up
